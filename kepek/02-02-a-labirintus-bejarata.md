# 2. fejezet – A Holdvári-labirintus bejárata

**Kapcsolódó mondat (2. fejezet, „A Holdvári-labirintus"):**
„A bejáratnál kőív állt, alatta a Játékmester, mellette az inas egy üres ezüsttálcával."

**Jelenet:** a kert a kastély mögött. A kamera a vendégek mögött áll, előttük a kőív a sövényfalban, az ív alatt a Játékmester és az inas. Az ív mögött köd.

## Prompt (angolul)

```
Monochrome graphite pencil drawing on cream paper, hand-hatched, soft smudged
shading, fine paper grain, soft vignette edges, ominous gothic mood.
Landscape, 3:2.

COMPOSITION: one-point perspective, symmetrical. The camera stands at standing
eye height, behind a group of people, looking at the entrance of a hedge
maze about fifteen meters away. Overcast morning, pale light, fog.

BACKGROUND, CENTER: a huge wall of dark green yew hedge, three meters high,
century-old, with branches so densely woven that nothing is visible behind it
except fog resting among the leaves. The hedge wall runs across the whole
width of the picture, edge to edge, its top edge level and a little higher
than the people. In the exact center of the hedge wall stands a weathered
stone arch, an entrance about three meters tall. Through the arch a narrow
gravel corridor between two hedge walls leads straight away from the camera
and vanishes into fog after a few meters.

UNDER THE ARCH: standing exactly in the middle of the arch opening, a very
tall man in a floor-length black cloak with a high stiff collar, face in deep
shadow, no face visible, hands hidden in his sleeves. Directly to his left, on
the viewer's right side of him, a footman in a black uniform and black peaked
cap stands rigid, holding in front of his waist a large round empty silver
tray, tilted a little toward the camera so that it is clearly empty.

FOREGROUND: on gravel, in the lower third of the picture, about twenty people
in period clothing from the 1930s to the 1970s stand in a loose half-circle
facing the arch. They are seen from behind and from the side; their faces are
mostly not visible. Distinct figures: at the front left, a man in a dark
business suit speaking to the others with one raised finger, as if
explaining a calculation; at the far right edge, a little apart from the
others, a very tall, powerfully built, bearded man in a light open-collared
shirt, standing with arms hanging by his sides, looking at the hedge.
The rest of the group is tightly gathered, some looking at the arch,
some at each other.

DO NOT: show the Játékmester's face, add color, show the castle, let the
footman hold anything but the empty tray, add readable text.
```

**Szereplők (név szerint):** a bal oldali, ujjat emelő férfi Neumann János, a jobb szélen álló Bud Spencer.
