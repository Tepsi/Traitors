# 9. fejezet – A kilencedik Kerekasztal

**Kapcsolódó mondat (9. fejezet, „A Kerekasztal"):**
„A Kapu elé lépett, a kezét a zsebébe dugta, és sokáig csak állt ott, a fekete ajtóval szemközt, mintha a szavakat kellene megtalálnia."

**Jelenet:** Brando a Kerekasztal termében, a búcsúbeszéde előtt. A széke hátratolva, üresen áll a kerek asztalnál; ő a fekete, kilincs nélküli ajtó előtt áll, háttal a teremnek.

## Prompt (angolul)

```
Monochrome graphite pencil drawing on cream paper, hand-hatched, soft smudged
shading, fine paper grain, soft vignette edges, ominous gothic mood.
Landscape, 3:2.

THE ROOM: a circular domed chamber, the vaulted ceiling showing faded patches
of gold that were once stars. In the center a huge round table of black oak.
Seven chairs stand evenly around the table, each facing the table, and an
eighth chair, slightly pushed back and turned, stands empty in the
foreground. In front of each place a small slate and a stub of chalk. In the
curved wall directly opposite the camera, a tall black door with a thin
silver frame and no handle, no keyhole, the Gate of Oblivion, closed.

THE SEATED: seven figures sit at the table with their backs or sides to the
camera, all turned toward the standing man and the Gate, kept soft and
slightly blurred: Gábor Zsazsa in a glamorous 1950s gown on the left, Audrey
Hepburn in a simple dark 1950s dress with short dark hair, Maria Callas with
a dark cloak over her shoulders, Salvador Dalí with a long waxed upturned
moustache, Bud Spencer, very tall and broad, Freddie Mercury in a dark
jacket, Géza Hofi in a dark suit. All seven are seated and still, none speaking,
their hands on the table.

THE STANDING MAN: Marlon Brando in a dark casual jacket, seen from behind and
slightly from the left, standing alone on the open flagstones between the
round table and the Gate, about four steps from the black door, both hands
pushed deep into his jacket pockets, his head a little lowered, shoulders
heavy and relaxed, not moving. His face is not visible.

COMPOSITION: camera low, behind the table at the seated people's eye level,
looking across the table and the empty pushed-back chair toward the Gate; the
standing man is small and in the exact center of the picture, framed by the
black door; strong perspective lines from the table's edge toward the Gate.

LIGHT: a handful of candles on the table throw a low warm light on the slates
and the seated figures' shoulders; the standing man is lit only from behind
his figure by the faint silver of the door frame; the rest of the chamber
falls into deep shadow.

DO NOT: show Brando's face, make the door open, give the door a handle, add
more than eight chairs in total, add color, add readable text.
```

**Szereplők:** Brando (hátulról, arc nélkül), Zsazsa, Hepburn, Callas, Dalí, Bud Spencer, Mercury, Hofi (ülve, elmosódva).
