# 8. fejezet – A nyolcadik konklávé

**Kapcsolódó mondat (8. fejezet, „A nyolcadik konklávé"):**
„A gyertya lángja, amely tegnap még egyenesen égett, most az üres szék felé hajlott, mintha a közelében keresne valakit, és az asztal lapján nem feküdt semmi, sem szürke lap, sem fekete, csak a viasz kis, kihűlt cseppjei, ahol tegnap még valaki az ujját tartotta."

**Jelenet:** a torony konklávészobája éjfélkor. A kőasztalnál három szék áll, kettőre csuklyás alak ült le, a harmadik üres. A gyertya lángja az üres szék felé hajlik, az asztalon csak a kihűlt viaszcseppek maradtak.

## Prompt (angolul)

```
Monochrome graphite pencil drawing on cream paper, hand-hatched, soft smudged
shading, fine paper grain, soft vignette edges, ominous gothic mood.
Landscape, 3:2.

THE ROOM: a bare round stone chamber at the top of a tower. Rough dark stone
walls, one narrow arched window high in the back wall, its glass covered in
fog, no moonlight coming through. In the left wall, a heavy oak door with iron
straps, standing open a hand's width onto a dark stairwell. The floor is
rough grey flagstone.

THE TABLE: in the exact center of the room a plain, rough-hewn square stone
table, nothing on its top except one single thick candle standing in the
middle of it, burning with a small flame, and around the candle a few small
hardened pools of cold, pale wax on the stone, in a ring where someone once
rested a finger. No paper, no slip, no card of any color.

THE CHAIRS: three plain wooden stools around the table, one on the left side,
one on the right side, one on the far side facing the camera. The stool on
the far side, directly facing the viewer, is empty.

COMPOSITION: camera at the open door, at a seated person's eye level, looking
across the table; the table and the candle are in the exact center of the
picture, the empty stool in the center background directly behind the
candle.

THE TWO FIGURES: seated on the left stool and the right stool, seen in profile
facing each other across the table, both wearing long dark hooded cloaks whose
deep cowls completely hide their faces; no skin, no features, no hands are
visible except two dark sleeves resting on the stone edge of the table. The
left figure sits upright and still; the right figure sits slightly forward, the
hood tilted a little toward the candle.

THE FLAME: the candle flame, which was straight yesterday, now leans clearly
and distinctly toward the empty stool, as if searching for someone near it.
The curl of smoke above the flame follows the same line.

LIGHT: only the leaning candle flame lights the scene: a small warm pool on the
stone table, the sides of the dark hoods, and the empty stool's seat; all
the rest of the chamber falls into deep black shadow, with the window a faint
grey arch.

DO NOT: show any face under the hoods, put anything on the table other than the
candle and the wax drops, put a person on the third stool, add color, add
readable text.
```

**Szereplők:** a konklávé szereplői névtelenek (csuklya alatt), a prompt sem nevezi meg őket.
