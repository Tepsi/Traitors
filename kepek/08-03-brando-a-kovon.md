# 8. fejezet – A Kerekasztal

**Kapcsolódó mondat (8. fejezet, „A Kerekasztal"):**
„Brando lecsúszott a székről a padlóra, és ott ült a kövön, az arcát a tenyerébe temetve, és nevetett és sírt egyszerre, előbb hangtalanul, a vállát rázva, aztán hangosan, megkönnyebbülten."

**Jelenet:** a Kerekasztal terme közvetlenül azután, hogy a Kapu becsukódott a kiesett mögött. A kilenc megmaradt vendég a megkönnyebbülés minden formáját mutatja: Zsazsa a hátraborult székétől a Kapu felé rohant, aztán megtorpant és visszafordult; Brando a kőpadlón ül; Callas mozdulatlanul sír; Mercury a feje fölé kapott kézzel; Hofi a vállára borul; Bud Spencer széttárt karral ölelte meg Einsteint; Hepburn a szájához szorítja a kezét; Dalí egyenesen áll és tapsol.

## Prompt (angolul)

```
Monochrome graphite pencil drawing on cream paper, hand-hatched, soft smudged
shading, fine paper grain, soft vignette edges, ominous gothic mood, but the
emotion of the scene is overwhelming relief. Landscape, 3:2.

THE ROOM: a circular domed chamber of dark stone, faded patches of gold on
the vaulted ceiling. In the center a huge round table of black oak, a ring of
chairs around it, slates and chalk on the table. At the far wall, directly
opposite the camera, a tall black door with a silver frame and no handle,
now firmly closed: the Gate of Oblivion. Nobody stands near it except one
woman. The stone floor is dark flagstone.

COMPOSITION: camera at the entrance of the chamber, slightly raised, looking
across the round table toward the closed Gate. The table runs across the
middle of the picture; the nine people are scattered around it and on the
floor in a wide arc. A tenth chair is empty, pushed in neatly, at the center
of the table's far side, directly in front of the Gate.

THE NINE (each with a different reaction):
- in the left foreground, ON THE FLOOR beside the table, sitting on the stone
  with his legs bent and his back against a chair leg, a man in a dark casual
  jacket, his face buried in both hands, his shoulders shaking, laughing and
  crying at the same time (Marlon Brando), his chair behind him pushed back
  at an angle;
- at the left of the Gate, in mid-run, a woman in a glamorous 1950s gown with
  many bracelets, both hands pressed to her face, her body half turned back
  toward the room as if she doesn't know which way to run; behind her an
  overturned chair lying on its back (Gábor Zsazsa);
- seated very upright at the table, a woman with a dark cloak over her
  shoulders, perfectly motionless, one single tear on her cheek, no sound
  (Maria Callas);
- seated at the table, a man in a dark jacket with dark hair and a moustache,
  both hands thrown above his head, a half-length piece of chalk still in one
  fist, sobbing and laughing, his mouth open (Freddie Mercury);
- beside him, a man in a dark suit leaning over onto Mercury's shoulder with
  a wry, tired half-smile, dry as ever (Hofi Géza);
- in the center right, a very tall, powerful bearded man in a plain
  open-collared light shirt, both arms flung wide, embracing an old man in a
  rumpled cardigan with untidy white hair, who has a tired faint smile and
  is letting himself be held (Bud Spencer and Albert Einstein);
- seated at the table at right, a slim woman in a simple elegant dark 1950s
  dress with short dark hair, one hand pressed to her mouth, eyes wet (Audrey
  Hepburn);
- standing straight at the far right, a man in a dark suit with a long, thin,
  waxed upturned moustache, a cane tucked under one arm, his palms meeting in
  slow theatrical applause like the end of a performance (Salvador Dalí).

LIGHT: pale grey light falling from the dome onto the table and the figures
in the middle; the floor in the foreground in deep grey shadow; the Gate a
black rectangle with a thin silver outline.

DO NOT: show the man who has just left, add a Game Master, add color, show
anyone injured, add readable writing on the slates, show a handle on the Gate.
```

**Szereplők:** a nevek a promptban is szerepelnek.
