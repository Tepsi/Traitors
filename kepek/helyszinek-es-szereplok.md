# Helyszínek és szereplők: rögzített leírások

A képek akkor lesznek egységesek, ha a helyszín és a ruhák minden képen ugyanazok. Ezeket a leírásokat a promptok is tartalmazzák, mert a külső generátor nem emlékszik az előző képre. Új helyszínt vagy szereplőt először ide vegyünk fel, utána kerülhet promptba.

## Helyszínek

**A Nagyterem (Great Hall)**
A long rectangular Tudor hall, about thirty meters long, dark oak paneling on every wall, a high ceiling of exposed black timber beams, tall narrow leaded windows in the left long wall with fog pressing against the glass. One single long banquet table runs down the exact center of the hall along its length, covered with white linen. Twenty-two high-backed carved oak chairs: eleven along each long side, each pushed in so that the seat faces the table and the chair back faces the side wall, so the guests sit facing each other across the table. No chairs at the two short ends. In front of each place: a small gilded name plate, a plate, silver cutlery, a folded napkin. Silver candelabras with tall candles stand along the middle of the table. At the far short end of the hall, high on the wall, a stone balcony with a stone balustrade, reached by a curved stone staircase that descends into the hall along the right wall.

**A Kerekasztal terme (Round Table Chamber)**
A circular domed chamber in the east wing. The vaulted ceiling shows faded patches of gold, once stars. In the center a huge round table of black oak, so wide that people on opposite sides would have to shout. Twenty-one chairs spaced evenly all around it, each chair facing the table, and in front of each a small slate and a piece of chalk. In the chamber wall opposite the entrance stands a tall black door with a silver frame and no handle, the Gate of Oblivion.

**A Csend Szalonja (Salon of Silence)**
A square salon in the east wing. Heavy dark green velvet curtains drawn over every window, a thick dark patterned carpet that swallows all sound, dark paneled walls. In the exact center a huge black grandfather clock, about three meters tall, a silver pendulum visible behind a glass pane. Around it a perfect circle of nineteen chairs, all facing inward toward the clock.

**A torony konklávészobája (conclave chamber)**
A bare round stone chamber at the top of the north tower. Rough stone walls, one narrow arched window with fog against the glass, a heavy oak door with iron straps, a plain rough-hewn square stone table in the middle, three simple wooden stools around it. A single candle on the table is the only light.

**A Holdvári-labirintus (hedge maze)**
A maze of dark green yew hedge three meters high, a hundred years old, the branches so densely woven that nothing is visible behind the wall but fog resting among the leaves. The entrance is a weathered stone arch set into the hedge wall. In the center of the maze is a round grass clearing with a stone column; on top of the column is mounted a black iron shield with a crescent moon on it, and beside it a bronze bell hangs from an iron bracket.

**A Kútház (well house) és a lépcső**
The back courtyard of the castle, wet grey flagstones, always in fog. A small well house of grey stone with a heavy timber beam roof, open at the front, on the higher ground at the left; inside, a stone well rim with a pulley and a rope; twenty wooden buckets in a row along the wall. Forty paces away, on the lower ground at the right, a wide copper basin beside a wrought-iron garden gate. Between them seven narrow wet moss-covered stone steps descending from the well toward the basin.

**A Nyugati Galéria (West Gallery)**
A very long, narrow, dark gallery, the longest room of the castle. The floor is black and white marble tiles in an irregular scattered pattern, as if a chessboard had been shuffled, the lines between the tiles barely visible. Heavy dark velvet curtains cover the tall windows along one long wall. Along the opposite long wall, high above the floor at first-floor height, runs a narrow stone balcony with a stone balustrade, from which the whole floor can be overlooked. Tall candles in iron holders stand along the walls, so far apart that their light never touches. At the far end, a single bronze bell hangs from a thick rope, with a plain wooden chair beside it.

**A könyvtár (library)**
A library with heavy oak bookshelves from floor to ceiling, packed with leather-bound volumes. Tall windows in one wall let in grey fog light from the garden. Upholstered armchairs along the walls, an anonymous guest in each. In one shelf, an arm's length gap of lighter, cleaner wood where a long row of books once stood.

**A Mérlegterem (Hall of the Scale)**
A cold, tall, bare hall in the east wing, dark timber beams across a high ceiling, rough grey stone walls, dark flagstone floor, one tall narrow window letting in pale fog light, a heavy oak door with iron straps. In the center an enormous brass scale on a brass column, so large that a man could stand in one pan: the left pan gilded gold, the right pan black iron, each closed by a thick brass lid with one narrow slot, the beam held level by an iron pin. In front of it, between door and scale, a tall black lacquered folding screen. On a small table at the wall thirteen plain grey stones.

## Állandó szereplők

**Szerafin Barnabás, a Játékmester:** very tall, gaunt, upright, in a floor-length black cloak with a high stiff collar and wide sleeves, long pale hands. His face is always in deep shadow or hidden by the collar, never shown. Slow, solemn posture.

**Az inas (the footman):** black uniform with a high collar, black peaked cap, white gloves, rigid and motionless like a statue, face neutral.
