# 6. fejezet – A Kerekasztal

**Kapcsolódó mondat (6. fejezet, „A Kerekasztal"):**
„Hölgyeim és uraim: áruló vagyok."

**Jelenet:** a Kerekasztal termében Hitchcock a Feledés Kapuja előtt áll, szembe a teremmel, és kimondja a búcsúbeszéde csattanóját. A kapu még zárva van. Az asztalnál tizenkét vendég ül, mind őt nézik, Hitchcock saját széke üresen, hátratolva áll.

## Prompt (angolul)

```
Monochrome graphite pencil drawing on cream paper, hand-hatched, soft smudged
shading, fine paper grain, soft vignette edges, ominous gothic mood.
Landscape, 3:2.

THE ROOM: a circular domed chamber. The vaulted ceiling shows faded patches
of gold, once stars. In the center a huge round table of black oak. Thirteen
high-backed chairs stand evenly spaced all around it, every chair facing the
table, every sitter facing inward toward the middle. In front of each place
lies a small slate and a piece of chalk. In the chamber wall on the far side
from the camera stands a tall black door with a silver frame and no handle,
the Gate of Oblivion. The door is closed.

COMPOSITION: camera at the entrance side of the chamber, at a seated person's
eye level, looking straight across the round table toward the closed door on
the far wall. The door is exactly in the center of the picture. Think of the
table as a clock face with the camera at 6 o'clock and the door at 12 o'clock.

CENTER, standing on the floor right in front of the closed black door, small
against its silver frame: Alfred Hitchcock, in a formal dark three-piece suit,
his hands clasped loosely in front of him, a calm, faintly amused half-smile,
his gaze sweeping slowly across the table from face to face like a director
reviewing a cast. His posture is relaxed and theatrical, a man who has just
delivered the last line of a scene.

BESIDE THE DOOR, on the RIGHT of the doorframe from the viewer: a taller chair,
higher than the others, in which the Game Master sits, a very tall gaunt man
in a floor-length black cloak with a high stiff collar, his face in deep
shadow, long pale hands on the arms of the chair, motionless.

AROUND THE TABLE, twelve figures seated, mostly seen from behind or in
three-quarter view, all with their faces turned toward Hitchcock, none of
them moving, stunned silence:
- at 4 o'clock, right foreground, a woman in a glamorous 1950s gown with many
  bracelets, an open notebook in her hands, her mouth slightly open
  (Gábor Zsazsa);
- at 2 o'clock, right side, a man in a precise dark business suit, hands flat
  on the tabletop, his face cold and still (Neumann János);
- at 9 o'clock, left side, a woman in a simple elegant dark 1950s dress with
  short dark hair, her eyes lowered, her hands in her lap (Audrey Hepburn);
- at 10 o'clock, upper left, a man in a dark 1930s suit and bow tie, one
  hand pressed to his mouth (Kabos Gyula);
- the other eight are seated figures in 1930s to 1970s clothes, hands near
  their slates, faces in shadow.
The chair at about 7 o'clock, close to the camera in the left foreground, is
empty and pushed back from the table, with a small slate and a piece of chalk
lying in front of it, a faint chalk mark on the slate.

LIGHT: dim and warm from low lamps on the table, long shadows up the walls;
the silver frame of the closed door catches a cold glint behind Hitchcock.

DO NOT: add more or fewer than thirteen chairs, turn the chairs outward, show
the Game Master's face, give the door a handle, open the door, add color, add
readable text on the slates or in the notebook.
```

**Szereplők:** a nevek a promptban is szerepelnek.
