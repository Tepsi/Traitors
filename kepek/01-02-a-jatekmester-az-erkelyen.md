# 1. fejezet – A Játékmester az erkélyen

**Kapcsolódó mondat (1. fejezet, „A Nagyterem"):**
„Egyszerre kialudtak a gyertyák. A terem végében, az erkélyen egyetlen fénysugár villant fel, és a fényben egy magas, fekete köpenyes férfi állt."

**Jelenet:** a Nagyterem hosszú asztalát a rövid vége felől nézzük végig. A vendégek az asztal két oldalán ülnek, egymással szemben, oldalnézetben, és mind a terem távoli vége felé, az erkélyre fordították a fejüket. Az erkélyen fénysugárban áll a Játékmester.

## Prompt (angolul)

```
Monochrome graphite pencil drawing on cream paper, hand-hatched, soft smudged
shading, fine paper grain, soft vignette edges, ominous gothic mood, strong
chiaroscuro. Landscape, 3:2.

COMPOSITION: one-point perspective, perfectly symmetrical. The camera stands
at the near short end of a very long banquet table, at seated eye height,
looking straight down the length of the table toward the far end of the hall.
The table runs from the bottom center of the picture to the vanishing point in
the center. It is covered with a straight white linen cloth.

THE HALL: a long Tudor great hall, dark oak paneling on every wall, a high
ceiling of exposed black timber beams, tall narrow leaded windows in the left
wall with fog pressing against the glass. Down the middle of the table stand
silver candelabras; every candle has just gone out and thin wisps of smoke
curl up from the wicks. In front of each place a small gilded name plate.

THE GUESTS: twenty-two people in period evening clothes from the 1930s to the
1970s, eleven along the left side of the table and eleven along the right
side. Each sits on a high-backed carved oak chair that is pushed in to the
table: the seat faces the table and the chair back faces the side wall. So the
left row sits facing right and the right row sits facing left, the two rows
face each other across the table, and the camera sees every guest in side
profile. No chair stands at the near end or at the far end of the table.
Every guest has turned their head toward the far end of the hall and looks up
at the balcony, so all the heads in both rows point the same way, toward the
vanishing point. The nearest guests are large and almost black against the
light, the farthest are small. Faces only dimly visible.

THE BALCONY: on the far short wall, high up, centered above the far end of
the table, a stone balcony with a stone balustrade. From high above, one
narrow, hard beam of pale light falls straight down onto the center of the
balcony. In the beam stands a very tall man in a floor-length black cloak with
a high stiff collar, motionless, arms hidden inside wide sleeves, his face in
deep shadow (no face visible). Behind him pure darkness. A curved stone
staircase leads down from the balcony along the right wall.

LIGHT: the hall is dark except for the single beam on the balcony and the
faint pale glow of the fog in the windows. The white tablecloth catches a
little light and leads the eye to the balcony.

DO NOT: put chairs at the ends of the table, turn the chairs away from the
table, show the cloaked man's face, add color, add readable text, add modern
objects.
```
