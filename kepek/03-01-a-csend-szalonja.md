# 3. fejezet – A Csend Szalonja

**Kapcsolódó mondat (3. fejezet, „A Csend Szalonja"):**
„A terem közepén hatalmas álló óra állt, fekete tokjában ezüst ingával, és körülötte tizenkilenc szék kört alkotott."

**Jelenet:** a szalon felülről nézve, a tizenkilenc szék köre befelé, az óra felé fordul. Mindenki némán ül, mindenkinek más a jellegzetes mozdulata. Az egyik szék mögött megáll a Játékmester.

## Prompt (angolul)

```
Monochrome graphite pencil drawing on cream paper, hand-hatched, soft smudged
shading, fine paper grain, soft vignette edges, ominous gothic mood.
Landscape, 3:2.

THE ROOM: a square salon. Heavy dark green velvet curtains hang over every
window, a thick dark patterned carpet covers the floor, the walls are dark
paneling. Everything swallows sound.

COMPOSITION: a high camera angle, as if seen from a gallery or the top of a
doorframe at one edge of the room, looking down at about forty-five degrees
toward the center of the room.

CENTER: in the exact center of the picture stands a huge black grandfather
clock, about three meters tall, a silver pendulum visible behind a glass pane
in the case. Around the clock, a perfect circle of nineteen chairs. Every
chair faces INWARD toward the clock, so that every sitter faces the clock;
we see the sitters nearest to us from behind and the sitters on the far side
of the ring from the front. A figure sits on every chair. Nobody speaks.

THE SITTERS, in period clothing from the 1930s to the 1970s. Distinct figures
(positions as on a clock face seen from above, 6 o'clock is nearest to the
camera):
- at 7 o'clock: a woman in an evening gown with many sparkling bracelets sitting
  stiffly;
- at 8 o'clock, seen from behind and the side: a man in a dark suit with a very
  long, thin, upturned waxed moustache, gripping the moustache with both
  hands, mouth pressed shut;
- at 10 o'clock: a woman in a dark cape with one hand raised to her throat, her
  chin lifted, breathing slowly like a trained singer;
- at 12 o'clock, far side, facing the camera: a man in a formal dark
  three-piece suit with eyes closed and a faint smile at the corner of his mouth;
- at 1 o'clock: a very tall bearded man in an open-collared shirt, sitting
  perfectly relaxed with his arms folded, doing nothing, quite at ease;
- at 3 o'clock: a man with untidy white hair and a rumpled cardigan, sticking
  his tongue out;
- at 4 o'clock: a man in a dark casual jacket with closed eyes, perfectly calm.
The other chairs hold anonymous seated figures in similar clothes, hands in
their laps, eyes on the clock or on the floor.

THE CLOAKED MAN: right behind the chair of the woman at 7 o'clock, outside the
ring, a very tall man in a floor-length black cloak with a high stiff collar
has stopped. His face is in deep shadow and not visible. One pale hand hovers
just above her shoulder without touching it. Behind him, at the room's edge,
a footman in a black uniform and cap stands rigid in the shadows.

LIGHT: dim, warm, with long soft shadows; the clock case is the darkest
shape in the picture, the pendulum catches a little light.

DO NOT: turn the chairs outward, add more or fewer than nineteen chairs, show
the cloaked man's face, add color, add readable text on the clock face.
```

**Szereplők (név szerint):** 7 óránál Elizabeth Taylor, 8 óránál Salvador Dalí, 10 óránál Maria Callas, 12 óránál Alfred Hitchcock, 1 óránál Bud Spencer, 3 óránál Albert Einstein, 4 óránál Marlon Brando.
