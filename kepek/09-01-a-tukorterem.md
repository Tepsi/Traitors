# 9. fejezet – A Tükörterem

**Kapcsolódó mondat (9. fejezet, „A Tükörterem"):**
„A terem közepén kőpárkány állt, a tetején sárgaréz láda, két tenyérnyi, keskeny réssel a fedelén."

**Jelenet:** a déli szárny hosszú tükörterme. Mindkét oldalon négy-négy magas, aranykeretes tükör, a falak mentén nyolc sötét fa fülke, a terem közepén a kőpárkányon a sárgaréz láda, mellette a Játékmester. A belépő nyolc vendég arca homályosan tükröződik.

## Prompt (angolul)

```
Monochrome graphite pencil drawing on cream paper, hand-hatched, soft smudged
shading, fine paper grain, soft vignette edges, ominous gothic mood.
Landscape, 3:2.

THE ROOM: a long, narrow Tudor hall, longer than the building should allow,
dark oak floor, dark beamed ceiling fading into shadow at the far end. A
heavy double door of dark oak stands open behind the camera. Along the LEFT
long wall four tall mirrors in tarnished gilded frames, and along the RIGHT
long wall four more, all eight with spotted, clouded glass. Between the
mirrors, along both walls, eight dark wooden booths in a row, each with a
heavy velvet curtain pulled half aside; inside each booth a tiny lamp and a
narrow gilt-framed mirror at eye height on the back wall.

THE CENTER: on the exact center line of the hall, a squat square stone
pedestal, and on top of it a heavy brass chest about two hand-widths wide,
with one narrow slit in its lid, like a letter box. Nothing else on the
pedestal.

THE GAME MASTER: standing right beside the pedestal, very tall, gaunt,
upright, in a floor-length grey cloak with a high stiff collar and wide
sleeves, long pale hands resting on the edge of the chest. His face is in
deep shadow behind the collar, never shown.

THE EIGHT GUESTS: just entering through the door in the foreground, seen from
behind and slightly from the side, spread in a loose group at the bottom of
the picture: Marlon Brando in a dark casual jacket, Audrey Hepburn in a
simple dark 1950s dress with short dark hair, Salvador Dalí in a dark suit
with a long waxed upturned moustache, Bud Spencer, very tall and broad, in a
plain open-collared light shirt, Maria Callas with a dark cloak over her
shoulders, Gábor Zsazsa in a glamorous 1950s gown, Géza Hofi in a dark suit,
Freddie Mercury in a dark jacket. Eight small, slightly blurred faces look
back at them out of the mirrors, one in each of several mirrors, pale and
unfocused.

COMPOSITION: camera low, at the doorway, looking straight down the middle of
the hall; the pedestal with the brass chest and the grey figure in the center
of the picture, mirrors converging toward a vanishing point behind it; the
candle flames repeated two or three times in each mirror as small drifting
lights.

LIGHT: tall candles in iron stands along the walls, their small flames
multiplied in the mirrors; a faint pool of light on the brass chest; the far
end of the hall dissolves into dark.

DO NOT: add color, add readable text, show the Game Master's face, put any
other object on the pedestal, make the hall short or square, add chairs or
tables.
```

**Szereplők:** Brando, Hepburn, Dalí, Bud Spencer, Callas, Zsazsa, Hofi, Mercury (hátulról, apró alakok); a Játékmester arc nélkül.
