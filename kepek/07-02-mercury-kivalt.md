# 7. fejezet – A váltságdíj

**Kapcsolódó mondat (7. fejezet, „A váltságdíj"):**
„Maria Callast váltom ki."

**Jelenet:** a Nagyteremben az asztalt félretolták. A falon négy keskeny fehér tábla függ egy-egy névvel, alattuk kis faasztalon a fekete könyv, a toll és a tinta. A nyolc kint maradó vendég félkörben áll, Mercury az utolsó helyről előlép, és kimondja a döntését. A Játékmester a könyv mellett áll.

## Prompt (angolul)

```
Monochrome graphite pencil drawing on cream paper, hand-hatched, soft smudged
shading, fine paper grain, soft vignette edges, ominous gothic mood.
Landscape, 3:2.

THE ROOM: a long rectangular Tudor hall, dark oak paneling on every wall, a
high ceiling of exposed black timber beams, tall narrow leaded windows in the
left long wall with fog pressing against the glass. The long banquet table
has been pushed to the side and stands against the right wall, with the high-
backed chairs stacked or pushed up against it, so the middle of the hall is
an empty stretch of bare dark floor. At the far short end of the hall, high
on the wall, a stone balcony with a stone balustrade.

COMPOSITION: camera at the entrance end of the hall at standing eye level,
looking down the empty floor toward the far short wall. The far wall is in the
center of the picture.

ON THE FAR WALL, below the balcony, four narrow white boards hang side by
side at equal height, each with a short line of black lettering that is only
an illegible scrawl, no readable words.

BELOW THE BOARDS, slightly to the RIGHT of center: a small plain wooden table
with a thin black book lying open on it, a pen beside it and a small bottle
of ink. Behind the table, standing very straight, the Game Master in his
black cloak with a high stiff collar, his face in deep shadow, one pale long
hand resting on the open book, the other holding the pen.

IN THE MIDDLE GROUND: eight people stand in a tight half-circle facing the
boards, close together, seen mostly from behind or in three-quarter view,
like cold people huddling:
- at the far left of the half-circle: a man in a dark suit with his hands in
  his pockets (Hofi Géza);
- beside him a very tall powerful man with a thick dark beard in a plain
  open-collared light shirt with rolled sleeves (Bud Spencer);
- a man in a dark suit with a very long thin waxed upturned moustache, a
  walking stick in his hand, chin raised (Salvador Dalí);
- a woman in a simple elegant dark 1950s dress with short dark hair, her
  hands clasped at her waist (Audrey Hepburn);
- a man in a dark casual jacket, his head bowed, relaxed posture (Marlon
  Brando);
- an older man with untidy white hair in a rumpled cardigan (Albert
  Einstein);
- a man in a precise dark business suit, hands folded in front of him, still
  and cold (Neumann János);
- STEPPING FORWARD out of the half-circle toward the boards, a single pace
  ahead of the others, near the CENTER of the picture, with one hand lifted
  halfway to his lips and his head high: a man in a dark jacket with dark
  hair and a moustache (Freddie Mercury).

LIGHT: pale grey fog light from the tall windows on the left, a single warm
candle glow near the Game Master's table; the white boards catch the light
and are the brightest area of the picture; long soft shadows across the
floor.

DO NOT: show the Game Master's face, make any lettering readable, add
chairs in the middle of the floor, add more or fewer than eight people in the
half-circle, add color.
```

**Szereplők:** a nevek a promptban is szerepelnek.
