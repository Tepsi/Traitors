# 8. fejezet – A Kerekasztal

**Kapcsolódó mondat (8. fejezet, „A Kerekasztal"):**
„Einstein volt az, aki felállt."

**Jelenet:** a Kerekasztal terme a nyolcadik este. Tízen ülnek a kerek asztalnál, a palatáblák fordítva előttük, a kréta mellettük. Einstein hátratolta a székét, és előregörnyedve áll, a kardigánját összehúzva, mert ezt eddig senki sem várta tőle (eddig mindig ülve beszélt). A terem végében a falba mélyedve a Feledés Kapuja.

## Prompt (angolul)

```
Monochrome graphite pencil drawing on cream paper, hand-hatched, soft smudged
shading, fine paper grain, soft vignette edges, ominous gothic mood.
Landscape, 3:2.

THE ROOM: a circular domed chamber of dark stone. The vaulted ceiling shows
faded patches of gold, once stars, barely visible. In the center a huge round
table of black oak. Ten high-backed chairs stand evenly spaced all around it,
each facing the table, and in front of each place a small slate lying face
down and a short piece of chalk beside it. At the far wall, directly opposite
the camera, set deep into the stone, stands a tall black door with a silver
frame and no handle, no lock, perfectly still: the Gate of Oblivion.

COMPOSITION: camera at the entrance of the chamber, slightly raised, looking
across the round table toward the Gate in the exact center of the far wall.
The ring of ten chairs is seen in perspective: the nearest chairs at the left
and right edges of the picture with backs partly to the camera, the far ones
in the middle ground facing the viewer. The Gate fills the central top of the
picture.

THE STANDING MAN: in the middle of the left side of the table, a chair has
been pushed back, and in front of it stands an old man, bent slightly forward,
the front of his rumpled cardigan pulled closed with both hands, his untidy
white hair falling over his forehead, his gaze lowered and serious as if
searching for the first word (Albert Einstein). He is the only person
standing. His empty chair is behind him, its back toward the camera.

THE NINE SEATED: each in front of a slate and chalk, all faces turned toward
the standing man: a very tall powerful bearded man in a plain open-collared
shirt with his arms on the table (Bud Spencer); a woman with a dark cloak over
her shoulders sitting very upright (Maria Callas); a man with dark hair and a
moustache in a dark jacket, his shoulders trembling slightly (Freddie
Mercury); a man in a dark suit with a closed, unsmiling face (Hofi Géza); a man
in a dark suit with a long thin waxed upturned moustache, a cane leaning
against his chair (Salvador Dalí); a man in a dark casual jacket with his arms
crossed over his chest (Marlon Brando); a man in a precise dark business suit
sitting perfectly still, his eyes lowered to the tabletop, his face
motionless (Neumann János), seated directly across the table from the
standing man; a slim woman in a simple elegant dark 1950s dress with short
dark hair, her hands folded on the table (Audrey Hepburn); a woman in a
glamorous 1950s gown with many bracelets, her eyes deliberately fixed on the
far wall, away from the man in the precise suit (Gábor Zsazsa).

LIGHT: pale grey light from high in the dome falls on the center of the table
and on the standing man's white hair; the faces of the seated figures are in
soft half shadow; the Gate is a deep black rectangle with a thin silver
outline.

DO NOT: put a second standing person, add a handle or lock to the Gate, add
readable writing on the slates, add color, show a Game Master.
```

**Szereplők:** a nevek a promptban is szerepelnek.
