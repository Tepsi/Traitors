# 8. fejezet – Az Emlékezet Próbája

**Kapcsolódó mondat (8. fejezet, „Az Emlékezet Próbája"):**
„A terem közepén egy szürke bársonnyal letakart asztal állt, rajta egy urna, két homokóra és egy pecsétes láda, amelynek a tetején két fekete boríték feküdt egymás mellett, az urnától távol, mintha nem tartoznának a többi közé."

**Jelenet:** a Nevek Terme, a nyugati szárny földszintjén. A falak mentén tizenkét üres fekete keret, mindegyik alatt kialudt gyertya és simára kopott rézlemez. A terem közepén a letakart asztal az urnával, a homokórákkal és a ládával. A terem végében a Játékmester áll, vele szemben tíz vendég sorakozik fel, és mind a tízen a keretek felé pillantanak.

## Prompt (angolul)

```
Monochrome graphite pencil drawing on cream paper, hand-hatched, soft smudged
shading, fine paper grain, soft vignette edges, ominous gothic mood.
Landscape, 3:2.

THE ROOM: a long, low, barrel-vaulted stone hall, a cold windowless room that
nobody has seen before, the vault lines running away from the viewer toward a
plain far wall. Along each of the two long side walls hang six empty black
rectangular picture frames in a single even row, twelve in total, each frame
a plain black wooden border with nothing inside it, only blank dark wall. Under
every frame an iron spike on the wall holds one thick unlit candle, its wick
black and cold, and beneath each candle a small brass plate with no letters on
it, only smooth worn metal.

THE TABLE: in the exact center of the hall a square table covered to the floor
by a heavy grey velvet cloth with soft folds. On it, from left to right as
seen from the camera: a tall plain clay urn with a narrow neck; two glass
hourglasses standing side by side, the sand not yet running; and a small
sturdy wooden chest with iron bands and a lump of wax seal on its latch. On
the lid of the chest two black envelopes lie side by side, flat, a hand's
width apart from the urn and from the hourglasses, as if they did not belong
with the rest.

COMPOSITION: camera at the entrance door at the back of the hall, at standing
eye level, looking straight down the length of the hall. The table is in the
middle ground. At the far end of the hall, in front of the plain end wall,
stands the Game Master, Szerafin Barnabás, very tall, gaunt and upright, in a
floor-length black cloak with a high stiff collar and wide sleeves, his long
pale hands hanging still at his sides, his face entirely in shadow behind the
collar. In front of him, between the camera and him, around the table's far
side, ten people stand in one straight line side by side, facing the Game
Master, so the viewer sees them from behind and slightly from the side. All
ten have their heads half turned toward the empty frames on the side walls,
though none looks directly at them.

THE TEN (left to right along the line, backs to the camera): a very tall,
powerful bearded man in a plain open-collared light shirt with rolled sleeves
(Bud Spencer); a woman with a dark cloak over her shoulders, very upright
(Maria Callas); a man in a dark jacket with dark hair and a moustache, his
open palms held out in front of him (Freddie Mercury); an old man in a
rumpled cardigan with untidy white hair (Albert Einstein); a man in a dark
suit (Hofi Géza); a man in a dark suit with a long, thin, waxed upturned
moustache, a cane in one hand (Salvador Dalí); a man in a dark casual jacket,
relaxed shoulders (Marlon Brando); a man in a precise dark business suit
standing very straight (Neumann János); a slim woman in a simple elegant dark
1950s dress with short dark hair (Audrey Hepburn); a woman in a glamorous
1950s gown with many bracelets, her hands pressed together at her waist
(Gábor Zsazsa).

LIGHT: no window; the only light is a cold pale glow from above that falls on
the grey velvet and on the black ranks of frames, so the frames read as
darker rectangles; the unlit candles give no light at all. The far end behind
the Game Master is the darkest.

DO NOT: add a window, show any name or text on the brass plates, put pictures
or faces inside the frames, light any candle, add more or fewer than twelve
frames, show the Game Master's face, add color.
```

**Szereplők:** a nevek a promptban is szerepelnek.
