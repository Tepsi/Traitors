# 7. fejezet – A Kerekasztal

**Kapcsolódó mondat (7. fejezet, „A Kerekasztal"):**
„Kabos felállt. A térde meginogott, de Puskás kinyújtotta a kezét, és megtartotta a könyökét."

**Jelenet:** a Kerekasztal termében Kabost kiszavazták, és felállt. A térde meginog, Puskás a szomszéd székből kinyújtott kézzel megtartja a könyökét. A teremben tizenkét vendég ül, mindenki feléjük néz, a Játékmester a magasabb székben ül, a fekete ajtó zárva.

## Prompt (angolul)

```
Monochrome graphite pencil drawing on cream paper, hand-hatched, soft smudged
shading, fine paper grain, soft vignette edges, ominous gothic mood.
Landscape, 3:2.

THE ROOM: a circular domed chamber. The vaulted ceiling shows faded patches
of gold, once stars. In the center a huge round table of black oak. Twelve
high-backed chairs stand evenly spaced all around it, every chair facing the
table, every sitter facing inward toward the middle. In front of each place
lies a small slate and a piece of chalk, some slates turned face down. In the
chamber wall on the far side from the camera stands a tall black door with a
silver frame and no handle, the Gate of Oblivion. The door is closed.

COMPOSITION: camera at the entrance side of the chamber, at a seated person's
eye level, looking across the round table toward the closed door on the far
wall. Think of the table as a clock face with the camera at 6 o'clock and the
door at 12 o'clock. The door is exactly in the center of the picture.

AT 3 O'CLOCK, right side of the picture, in the CENTER of attention, two
figures close together:
- a small man in a dark 1930s three-piece suit and bow tie has just risen from
  his chair, his chair pushed back behind him, his knees buckling, his body
  swaying forward, one hand grasping at the edge of the table, his face
  stricken, his mouth slightly open (Kabos Gyula);
- the man in the neighboring chair, in a dark 1950s suit, has half-risen and
  stretches one strong hand out to hold the small man by his elbow, calm,
  steady, his eyes on the small man's face (Puskás Ferenc).

NEAR THE DOOR, on the RIGHT of the doorframe from the viewer: a taller chair,
higher than the others, in which the Game Master sits, a very tall gaunt man
in a floor-length black cloak with a high stiff collar, his face in deep
shadow, long pale hands on the arms of the chair, motionless.

AROUND THE TABLE, the other ten seated figures, mostly seen from behind or in
three-quarter view, all with their faces turned toward the small standing man,
none of them moving:
- at 5 o'clock, right foreground, a woman in a glamorous 1950s gown with many
  bracelets, a notebook open in front of her, her hand pressed to her mouth
  (Gábor Zsazsa);
- at 8 o'clock, left side, a man in a precise dark business suit, his hands
  folded on the tabletop, his gaze cold and fixed (Neumann János);
- at 10 o'clock, upper left, a woman in a simple elegant dark 1950s dress with
  short dark hair, her eyes lowered, her hands clasped in her lap (Audrey
  Hepburn);
- at 9 o'clock, left, a woman in a dark cloak over a dark dress, upright and
  rigid (Maria Callas);
- the remaining six are seated figures in 1930s to 1970s clothes (among them a
  man in a dark suit with a long thin waxed upturned moustache, a very tall
  bearded man in a light open-collared shirt, an older man with untidy white
  hair in a rumpled cardigan, a man in a dark casual jacket, a man in a dark
  suit, a man in a dark jacket with a moustache), hands near their slates,
  faces half in shadow.

LIGHT: dim and warm from low lamps on the table, long shadows up the walls;
the silver frame of the closed door catches a cold glint.

DO NOT: add more or fewer than twelve chairs, turn the chairs outward, show
the Game Master's face, give the door a handle, open the door, add color, add
readable text on the slates or in the notebook.
```
