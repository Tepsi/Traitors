# 7. fejezet – A Kápolna

**Kapcsolódó mondat (7. fejezet, „A Kápolna"):**
„Négy pad volt benne, szorosan egymás mellett, egy keskeny, magas ablak, amelyen át a tél színtelen fénye csak egyetlen sávban érte el a padlót, és egy vaskos oltárasztal, amelyen négy vastag gyertya égett, mindegyik ugyanolyan magasságú."

**Jelenet:** a kert végében álló kis kőkápolna belseje. A négy listás vendég itt vár a délig, bezárva. Kabos az első padon ül, Puskás mellette, Zsazsa a harmadik padon a füzetével, Callas az oltárasztalnál áll a gyertyák közelében.

## Prompt (angolul)

```
Monochrome graphite pencil drawing on cream paper, hand-hatched, soft smudged
shading, fine paper grain, soft vignette edges, ominous gothic mood.
Landscape, 3:2.

THE ROOM: a very small, bare chapel of rough grey stone with a low vaulted
ceiling, no decoration, no statues, no cross. Four plain wooden pews stand
close together in a row, each pew facing the altar at the far end, so the
sitters have their backs to the camera. In the far end wall, high above the
altar, a single narrow tall window of clear glass; a single band of pale
colorless winter light falls from it diagonally across the stone floor and
stops short of the pews. Under the window a heavy plain stone altar table
with four thick candles on it, all exactly the same height, all burning with
small straight flames.

COMPOSITION: camera at the closed entrance door at the back of the chapel,
at a seated person's eye level, looking straight down the narrow room toward
the altar and the window. The pews fill the middle ground; the altar and the
window are in the exact center of the picture. The door behind the camera is
not visible.

FOREGROUND and MIDDLE GROUND, four figures, three seated on pews seen from
behind or in three-quarter view, one standing:
- on the FIRST PEW, nearest the camera, left side: a small man in a dark
  1930s three-piece suit and bow tie, his shoulders hunched, hands clasped on
  his knees, his head slightly turned toward his neighbor, his fingers tense
  (Kabos Gyula);
- beside him on the same pew, to his RIGHT from the viewer: a sturdy man in a
  dark 1950s suit, relaxed and calm, one hand resting on the small man's
  shoulder, his gaze lifted toward the ceiling (Puskás Ferenc);
- on the THIRD PEW, right side: a woman in a glamorous 1950s gown with many
  bracelets, an open notebook lying on her lap, her pencil resting on the
  page, her face turned in profile toward the window (Gábor Zsazsa);
- STANDING at the altar table on the left, close to the candles, with her back
  half turned to the camera: a tall woman in a dark cloak over a dark dress,
  upright and still, her face almost entirely in shadow between the candle
  light and the dark fabric (Maria Callas).
The second pew and the fourth pew are empty.

LIGHT: the single band of pale window light on the floor; the four candle
flames warm and small on the altar; the rest of the room in deep grey
shadow, so the figures have their faces half hidden.

DO NOT: add a cross, statues, or stained glass, add more than four people,
turn the pews away from the altar, add color, add readable text in the
notebook.
```

**Szereplők:** a nevek a promptban is szerepelnek.
