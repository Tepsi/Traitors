# 5. fejezet – A Kerekasztal

**Kapcsolódó mondat (5. fejezet, „A Kerekasztal"):**
„Taylor megigazította a karperecét, a küszöbön megállt, és hátrafordult."

**Jelenet:** a Kerekasztal termében Taylor kilép a Feledés Kapuján, a küszöbön megáll, és visszafordul a teremhez. A köd a kapun túl szelíd és színtelen. Az asztalnál tizennégy vendég marad, Taylor széke üres.

## Prompt (angolul)

```
Monochrome graphite pencil drawing on cream paper, hand-hatched, soft smudged
shading, fine paper grain, soft vignette edges, ominous gothic mood.
Landscape, 3:2.

THE ROOM: a circular domed chamber. The vaulted ceiling shows faded patches
of gold, once stars. In the center a huge round table of black oak. Fifteen
high-backed chairs stand evenly spaced all around it, every chair facing the
table, every sitter facing inward toward the middle. In front of each place
lies a small slate and a piece of chalk. In the chamber wall on the far side
from the camera stands a tall black door with a silver frame and no handle,
the Gate of Oblivion. The door is now open inward, and beyond it there is
only pale, soft, featureless fog.

COMPOSITION: camera at the entrance side of the chamber, at a seated person's
eye level, looking straight across the round table toward the open door on the
far wall. The door is exactly in the center of the picture. Think of the
table as a clock face with the camera at 6 o'clock and the door at 12 o'clock.

CENTER, ON THE THRESHOLD of the open door, small against the pale fog behind
her: Elizabeth Taylor, in an elegant evening gown, many sparkling bracelets on
her wrists, turned back toward the room with her body half in the doorway,
her face lifted and calm, one hand just lowering from her other wrist where
she has straightened a bracelet. The fog outlines her in soft light.

BESIDE THE DOOR, on the RIGHT of the doorframe from the viewer: a taller chair,
higher than the others, in which the Game Master sits, a very tall gaunt man
in a floor-length black cloak with a high stiff collar, his face in deep
shadow, long pale hands on the arms of the chair, motionless.

AROUND THE TABLE, fourteen figures seated, mostly seen from behind or in
three-quarter view, all with their faces turned toward Taylor in the door,
none of them moving:
- at 10 o'clock, upper left, a white-haired man in a rumpled cardigan with his head
  bowed (Albert Einstein);
- at 3 o'clock, right side, a man in a dark casual jacket looking down at his own hands
  on the tabletop (Marlon Brando);
- the other twelve are anonymous seated figures in 1930s to 1970s clothes,
  hands near their slates, faces in shadow.
The chair at about 7 o'clock, close to the camera in the left foreground, is
empty and pushed back from the table, with a small slate and a piece of chalk
lying in front of it, a faint chalk mark on the slate.

LIGHT: dim and warm from low lamps on the table, long shadows up the walls;
the only cold, pale light in the picture comes from the fog beyond the open
door, rim-lighting Taylor and the silver frame of the door.

DO NOT: add more or fewer than fifteen chairs, turn the chairs outward, show
the Game Master's face, give the door a handle, show anything but fog beyond
the door, add color, add readable text on the slates.
```

**Szereplők:** a nevek a promptban is szerepelnek.
