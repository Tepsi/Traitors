# 4. fejezet – A Kerekasztal

**Kapcsolódó mondat (4. fejezet, „A Kerekasztal"):**
„Engedjék meg, hogy beszéljek."

**Jelenet:** a Kerekasztal termében Bessenyei felállt, a vitát lezáró beszédét mondja. A tizenhét szék az asztal körül, a Játékmester a magasabb székben.

## Prompt (angolul)

```
Monochrome graphite pencil drawing on cream paper, hand-hatched, soft smudged
shading, fine paper grain, soft vignette edges, ominous gothic mood.
Landscape, 3:2.

THE ROOM: a circular domed chamber. The vaulted ceiling shows faded patches
of gold, once stars. In the center a huge round table of black oak. Seventeen
high-backed chairs stand evenly spaced all around it, every chair facing the
table, every sitter facing inward toward the middle. In front of each place
lies a small slate and a piece of chalk. In the chamber wall on the far side
from the camera stands a tall black door with a silver frame and no handle,
the Gate of Oblivion.

COMPOSITION: a high camera angle from the entrance side, looking down at
about forty-five degrees across the table toward the black door on the far
wall.

FAR SIDE, in front of the black door: a taller chair, higher than the others.
In it sits the Game Master, a very tall gaunt man in a floor-length black cloak
with a high stiff collar, his face in deep shadow, long pale hands resting on
the arms of the chair, motionless.

LEFT SIDE of the table, at about 9 o'clock, standing: Ferenc Bessenyei, a
broad imposing man with silver hair in a dark formal suit, risen from his chair
with one fist set on the black tabletop, the other hand open, his head raised,
his mouth open in a deep, ringing voice, his gaze sweeping across the table.

AROUND THE TABLE, all seated, all turned toward Bessenyei:
- at 10 o'clock: Maria Callas, in a dark cape, chin lifted, lips pressed together;
- at 11 o'clock: Salvador Dalí, with his long thin waxed upturned moustache, one hand touching the moustache;
- at 12 o'clock, beside the Game Master: Bud Spencer, a very tall bearded man in an open-collared shirt, arms folded, calm;
- at 1 o'clock: Albert Einstein, untidy white hair and a rumpled cardigan, watching intently;
- at 3 o'clock, directly opposite Bessenyei: Alfred Hitchcock, in a formal dark three-piece suit, fingertips pressed together under his chin, a gentle faint smile;
- at 4 o'clock, near the camera, seen from behind and the side: Louis de Funès, in a rumpled dark suit, sitting on the very edge of his chair with both hands pressed against his knees, shoulders drawn up;
- at 5 o'clock: Elizabeth Taylor in an evening gown, a bracelet in her fingers, watching;
- at 6 o'clock, seen from behind: Audrey Hepburn in a simple dark dress, short dark hair, perfectly still.
The remaining chairs hold anonymous seated figures in 1930s to 1970s clothes,
all turned toward the standing man, hands near their slates.

LIGHT: dim and warm, a single low light from the lamps on the table casting
long shadows up the wall; the silver frame of the black door catches a thin
line of light.

DO NOT: add more or fewer than seventeen chairs, turn the chairs outward,
show the Game Master's face, make the door have a handle, add color, add
readable text on the slates.
```

**Szereplők:** a nevek a promptban is szerepelnek.
