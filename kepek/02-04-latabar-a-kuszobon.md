# 2. fejezet – Latabár a küszöbön

**Kapcsolódó mondat (2. fejezet, „A Kerekasztal"):**
„A küszöbön megbotlott, aztán hátrafordult, és Kabosra nézett, mintha az tehetne róla."

**Jelenet:** a Kerekasztal terme. A kamera az asztal mellett áll, közel Kabos Gyulához. A távolban, a kép jobb oldalán a Feledés Kapuja nyitva, mögötte fehér köd. A küszöbön Latabár, háromnegyed profilban hátrafordul.

## Prompt (angolul)

```
Monochrome graphite pencil drawing on cream paper, hand-hatched, soft smudged
shading, fine paper grain, soft vignette edges, melancholic and ominous mood.
Landscape, 3:2.

THE ROOM: a circular domed chamber. The vaulted ceiling shows faded patches of
gold, once stars. In the center stands a huge round table of black oak. Many
chairs stand around it, each facing the table, and in front of each place lies
a small slate with a piece of chalk.

COMPOSITION: the camera stands at the edge of the round table, at seated eye
height, looking across the room toward the door. The foreground, left third of
the picture: Kabos Gyula, in a dark 1930s three-piece suit and bow tie, sits at the table, seen in three-quarter view from the side and slightly behind, in
softer focus. His hands are held up in front of his chest as if he were
turning the brim of a hat, but there is no hat. He is looking toward the door
with a pained, helpless expression.

THE DOOR: at the far right of the picture, in the chamber wall, in the middle
distance, a tall black door framed in silver, with no handle, stands wide
open. Beyond it is soft, gentle, completely featureless white fog, like the
edge of the world. The fog glows and lights the doorway from behind.

THE MAN ON THE THRESHOLD: standing in the open doorway, in the center of the
fog light, Latabár Kálmán, in a dark 1950s suit and a neatly knotted tie. His right foot
has just caught on the doorsill, a small stumble in the middle of the step.
He has turned his upper body back toward the room and the camera, in
three-quarter view, and looks at the seated man in the foreground with a
bittersweet half-smile, a look that is both a joke and a farewell. The fog
makes a bright rim light around his head and shoulders.

THE ROOM IN BETWEEN: the round table with about a dozen seated figures in
period clothing, blurred, most sitting with bowed heads and looking at their
slates. They do not look at the door.

LIGHT: the room is dim with warm shadows; the only strong light comes from the
fog in the doorway. Gentle, not frightening.

DO NOT: show the door as scary, close the door, show color, show readable text
on the slates, put the standing man in the room.
```

**Szereplők:** a nevek a promptban is szerepelnek.
