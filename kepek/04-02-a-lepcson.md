# 4. fejezet – A lánc

**Kapcsolódó mondat (4. fejezet, „A Kútház"):**
„A lába megcsúszott."

**Jelenet:** a tizenhetedik vödörnél de Funès a lépcső első fokán elveszíti az egyensúlyát, a vödör kiesik a kezéből. Az egész lánc megdermed.

## Prompt (angolul)

```
Monochrome graphite pencil drawing on cream paper, hand-hatched, soft smudged
shading, fine paper grain, soft vignette edges, ominous gothic mood.
Landscape, 3:2.

THE PLACE: a flight of seven narrow wet stone steps with moss and a slick
shine, descending from LEFT (higher) to RIGHT (lower) between two grey stone
courtyard walls, thick fog in the background.

COMPOSITION: a low camera angle at the bottom right of the steps, looking
up-left along the stairway, so that the steps rise away from the camera
toward the upper left.

FOREGROUND, CENTER: Louis de Funès stands on the first (highest) step in a
rumpled dark 1960s suit with a loosened tie. His left foot has just slid
sideways off the wet edge of the step, his body tilted backwards, one arm
flung out for balance, his hair standing up, his famous expressive eyebrows
raised high, his mouth closed and tight, in complete silence. In his other
hand a half-empty wooden bucket is slipping out of his fingers, tilted, a thin
fine thread of water running from a hole in its side, a few droplets hanging
in the air.

LEFT, one step above and behind him, higher in the picture: Albert Einstein,
with untidy white hair and a rumpled cardigan, still with both arms stretched
forward in the gesture of having just handed over the bucket, his face frozen
in alarm. Behind Einstein, further left and higher: Salvador Dalí with his
long waxed upturned moustache, and further still, small and blurred in the
fog, the rest of the chain.

RIGHT, one step below and in front of de Funès, closer to the camera: Zsazsa
Gábor in a glamorous gown with many bracelets, both hands thrown forward to
catch the bucket, her bracelets swinging, her mouth open. Behind her, further
down the stairs to the right, other guests stand in line, every head turned
back toward de Funès, hands frozen in the middle of passing buckets.

BACKGROUND, top of the picture: in the fog above the stairs, barely visible,
the dark outline of a very tall cloaked figure, face hidden in shadow.

LIGHT: grey, flat, with a bright wet glint on the stone steps and on the
falling water; the figures are in half-shadow.

DO NOT: show the bucket already on the ground, show anyone falling over, add
blood, show the cloaked man's face, make the stairs go in the opposite
direction, add color, add readable text.
```

**Szereplők:** a nevek a promptban is szerepelnek.
