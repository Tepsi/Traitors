# 1. fejezet – A Holdvári-kastély

**Kapcsolódó mondat (1. fejezet, 2. bekezdés):**
„A Holdvári-kastély a dombtetőn állt, szürke kőből épült, tornyokkal és fekete gerendázatú szárnyakkal."

**Jelenet:** a kastély szemből, szimmetrikusan. Középen a szakállas gerendázatú oromzat és az ajtó, jobbra az ajtó mellett a lámpás és az inas, két oldalt egy-egy torony, mellettük a szárnyak.

**Fájlok:** `01-01-holdvari-kastely.png` (az én rajzom), `01-01-holdvari-kastely.svg` (vektoros forrás). Ha a generátor képet is elfogad referenciának, töltsd fel a rajzomat a pozíciók rögzítésére, és kérd, hogy a stílusa legyen ceruzarajz.

## Prompt (angolul)

```
Monochrome graphite pencil drawing on cream paper, hand-hatched, soft smudged
shading, fine paper grain, soft vignette edges, ominous gothic mood. Landscape,
3:2.

COMPOSITION: a perfectly symmetrical front view of a Tudor-style castle on a
gentle dome-shaped hilltop at night, camera on the ground slightly below the
castle, looking straight at it.

FOREGROUND: a pale gravel path winds in a gentle S-curve from the bottom
center of the picture up to the castle door. Dry grass tufts on both sides.
Low mist lies over the lower third of the picture and creeps along the path
toward the gate. Bare winter trees at the far left and far right edges.

CENTER: the central hall of the castle, grey stone ground floor, with a
pointed-arch heavy wooden door in the exact middle. Above it a jettied first
floor of black timber beams on pale plaster, and above that a steep
front-facing half-timbered gable with a dark roof edge. Directly to the right
of the door, standing on the ground in front of the wall, a wrought-iron
lantern on a tall iron post, burning with a small warm light that softly
lights the stones around it. Between the door and the lantern, standing
motionless like a statue, a footman in a black uniform and black peaked cap.

SIDES: to the left and right of the central hall, one round grey stone tower
each, with a tall conical dark slate roof and a few narrow slit windows; the
right tower is slightly taller than the left one. Beyond each tower a lower
wing with a stone ground floor, a black half-timbered upper floor, a dark
tiled roof and two tall brick chimneys.

WINDOWS: every single window is completely black, not one of them lit. Only
the lantern gives light.

SKY: heavy night sky filled with dense cross-hatching, getting lighter toward
the horizon, and a thin crescent moon in the upper right.

DO NOT: add color, add more people, light any window, add modern elements,
make the castle asymmetrical.
```
