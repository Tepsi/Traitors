# 6. fejezet – A hatodik konklávé

**Kapcsolódó mondat (6. fejezet, „A hatodik konklávé"):**
„A tölgyajtó kinyílt, és egy csuklyás alak lépett a kőasztalhoz, a kezében a fekete lappal."

**Jelenet:** az északi torony konklávészobájában két csuklyás áruló ül a kőasztalnál, köztük egyetlen gyertya a felére égett. Kinyílik a nehéz tölgyajtó, és a küszöbön egy harmadik csuklyás alak áll, kezében a fekete lappal, az ezüst pecséttel. A lépcsőn mögötte egy szürke alak sziluettje tűnik el. Az üres szék a néző oldalán áll.

## Prompt (angolul)

```
Monochrome graphite pencil drawing on cream paper, hand-hatched, soft smudged
shading, fine paper grain, soft vignette edges, ominous gothic mood.
Landscape, 3:2.

THE ROOM: a bare round stone chamber at the top of a tower. Rough stone
walls, one narrow arched window in the wall on the LEFT with fog pressing
against the glass, a heavy oak door with iron straps standing wide open
inward in the far wall, straight ahead of the camera. In the middle of the
room a plain, rough-hewn square stone table. Three simple wooden stools
stand around it: one on the left side of the table, one on the right side,
and one on the camera side, with its back to the viewer.

COMPOSITION: camera inside the chamber, at seated eye level, just behind
the empty stool, looking straight across the table toward the open door.
The door is exactly in the center of the picture, the table in the lower
middle.

CENTER, on the table: a single tall candle burned down to half its height,
the only light source in the picture. Beside it lie two flat sheets of
paper: one dark grey sheet, and a pen.

ON THE LEFT STOOL and ON THE RIGHT STOOL, seated facing each other across
the table in profile: two figures in long dark hooded robes, the hoods up and
the faces in complete shadow, nothing of the faces, hair or gender visible.
Their hands rest on the stone table, motionless. The one on the left sits
very upright with both hands spread flat on the stone; the one on the right is
leaning slightly forward, both hands clasped. The candle lights the underside
of their hoods and their hands.

THE EMPTY STOOL in the foreground, with its back to the camera, is pushed
slightly back as though someone has just stood up from it.

IN THE OPEN DOORWAY at the center of the picture, standing on the stone
threshold: a third hooded figure in a long dark robe, hood up, face in
complete shadow, holding a single black sheet of paper in both hands in front
of the chest, a round silver seal glinting on the sheet. The figure stands
still, one foot just over the threshold.

BEHIND THE DOORWAY, on the dark stairs: the faint, blurred outline of a tall
figure in a grey cloak, turning away and dissolving into the darkness of the
staircase, only a pale smudge.

LIGHT: only the candle; strong shadows climb the stone walls, the grey
fog light from the window on the left is very faint.

DO NOT: show any face, add more than three hooded figures in the room, make
the door closed, show writing on the sheets, add color, add a second candle,
add a fourth stool.
```

**Szereplők:** az árulók arca nem látszik, a nevük nem szerepel.
