# 7. fejezet – A váltságdíj

**Kapcsolódó mondat (7. fejezet, „A váltságdíj"):**
„Nem kértem, hogy fizessen értem valaki."

**Jelenet:** a Nagyterem ajtajában, a nyitott ajtó fényében megáll Callas, a kiváltott. A nyolc kint maradó vendég félkörben áll vele szemben, Mercury elöl, lesütött szemmel. Callas egyenesen, hidegen áll a küszöbön, és kimondja a mondatot.

## Prompt (angolul)

```
Monochrome graphite pencil drawing on cream paper, hand-hatched, soft smudged
shading, fine paper grain, soft vignette edges, ominous gothic mood.
Landscape, 3:2.

THE ROOM: a long rectangular Tudor hall seen from its far short end, dark oak
paneling on every wall, a high ceiling of exposed black timber beams, tall
narrow leaded windows in the left long wall with fog pressing against the
glass. The banquet table has been pushed against the right wall, so the
middle of the hall is an empty stretch of bare dark floor.

COMPOSITION: camera at the far short end of the hall at standing eye level,
looking down the empty floor toward the tall double door at the entrance end,
which stands open. The open doorway is exactly in the center of the picture;
bright pale daylight streams through it and cuts a sharp bright shape onto the
dark floor.

IN THE DOORWAY, small and upright against the bright light, in silhouette with
details only at the edges: a woman in a long dark dress with a dark cloak over
her shoulders, her back very straight, her chin slightly raised, her arms
rigid at her sides, her hands pressed to her hips; her face is composed and
cold (Maria Callas). Her shadow stretches long across the floor toward the
camera.

IN THE MIDDLE GROUND, between the camera and the doorway: eight people in a
loose half-circle with their backs mostly turned to the camera, all facing
the door, frozen:
- in front of the others, a single pace forward, a man in a dark jacket with
  dark hair and a moustache, his eyes lowered to the floor, one hand lifted
  halfway to his face (Freddie Mercury);
- around him, standing close: a man in a dark suit with his hands in his
  pockets (Hofi Géza); a very tall powerful bearded man in an open-collared
  shirt with rolled sleeves (Bud Spencer); a man in a dark suit with a very
  long thin waxed upturned moustache and a walking stick (Salvador Dalí); a
  woman in a simple dark 1950s dress with short dark hair (Audrey Hepburn); a
  man in a dark casual jacket (Marlon Brando); an older man with untidy white
  hair in a rumpled cardigan (Albert Einstein); a man in a precise dark
  business suit (Neumann János).

LIGHT: strong contrast; the doorway is the brightest area of the picture, the
hall is dim and grey with fog light from the left windows; the eight figures
are mostly dark shapes edged in light.

DO NOT: show the people's faces from the front except Callas in silhouette,
add more or fewer than eight people in the half-circle, add chairs in the
middle of the floor, add color, add readable text.
```

**Szereplők:** a nevek a promptban is szerepelnek.
