# 3. fejezet – Mercury tenyere

**Kapcsolódó mondat (3. fejezet, „A reggeli"):**
„A ceruzával írt név már nem látszott, csak egy halvány, szürke folt maradt a bőrén, mintha valaki letörölte volna."

**Jelenet:** Mercury a Nagyterem ajtajában megáll, és a tenyerére néz. A tenyér a kép előterében van, a háttérben elmosódva a hosszú asztal, Hitchcock és Bud Spencer.

## Prompt (angolul)

```
Monochrome graphite pencil drawing on cream paper, hand-hatched, soft smudged
shading, fine paper grain, soft vignette edges, quiet, sorrowful and ominous
mood. Landscape, 3:2.

COMPOSITION: close-up from just behind and above the shoulder of Freddie Mercury, who stands in a doorway, the camera at the height of his chest, looking forward
and slightly down at his own hand. The man himself is only suggested: the edge
of a dark jacket sleeve with a turned-back cuff, and a bit of his chin and
moustache at the very top edge of the picture, out of focus. He is not the
subject.

FOREGROUND, SHARP, lower center: an adult hand held open, palm turned up,
slender fingers beginning to curl closed, the lines of the palm drawn in
delicate hatching. On the palm there is only a faint, pale grey smudge, like
pencil writing that has been rubbed out. No letters can be read. The smudge
is drawn with soft rubbed graphite and looks as if someone had wiped the
skin.

BACKGROUND, DEEPLY BLURRED, seen through the open doorway: the Great Hall.
A long banquet table with white linen runs away from the camera down the
center of the hall, silver candelabras with lit candles along its middle, dark
oak paneling and tall narrow windows in the wall. At the table, in the
middle distance, two seated figures are recognizable even though blurred: on
the left side of the table Alfred Hitchcock, in a formal dark three-piece suit, sits with his hands clasped, looking at the doorway, and on the right side of the
table, Bud Spencer, a very tall, bearded man in a light shirt, sits next to a round black
iron shield that leans against his chair like an umbrella. The other chairs
are empty.

LIGHT: soft warm candlelight from the hall on the palm and the fingertips,
the doorway itself in shadow.

DO NOT: show any readable letters on the palm, show a full face, add color,
add more people.
```

**Szereplők:** a nevek a promptban is szerepelnek.
