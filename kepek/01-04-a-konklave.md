# 1. fejezet – Az első konklávé

**Kapcsolódó mondat (1. fejezet, „Az első konklávé"):**
„A gyertya lángja meghajolt, mintha valaki a háttérben sóhajtott volna."

**Jelenet:** az északi torony kerek kőszobája. Három csuklyás alak ül a kőasztalnál, az asztal közepén egyetlen gyertya. A lángja oldalra hajlik, a sötét szoba felé. Az arcuk nem látszik.

## Prompt (angolul)

```
Monochrome graphite pencil drawing on cream paper, hand-hatched, soft smudged
shading, fine paper grain, soft vignette edges, ominous gothic mood, strong
chiaroscuro. Landscape, 3:2.

COMPOSITION: the camera is slightly above table height, about two meters from
the table, looking at it from one side. The picture is dominated by a plain
rough-hewn square stone table in the middle of the frame.

THE ROOM: a bare round stone chamber at the top of a tower. Rough stone walls
curve behind the table. In the back wall, to the left, one narrow arched window
with fog pressed against the glass. In the back wall, to the right, a heavy
oak door with iron straps, standing slightly ajar, a thin line of darkness
along its edge. Cold stone floor.

THE CANDLE: exactly in the center of the table stands one single candle in a
plain iron holder. It is the only light source in the whole picture. Its
flame is bent sharply sideways, toward the dark room behind the table, as if
someone in the shadows had just sighed.

THE FIGURES: three figures in long dark hooded robes sit on three plain wooden
stools around the table, evenly spaced. One sits on the left of the table,
seen from the side; one sits on the far side of the table facing the camera;
one sits on the right of the table, seen from the side. Their hoods are
deep: the faces are completely hidden in pure black, no skin visible, no
eyes, no features. The only visible skin in the picture is three pairs of
pale hands: the hands of the far figure are clasped together on the table, the
other two pairs rest flat on the stone. They sit still and close together
around the candle.

LIGHT: the candle lights the hands, the table and the lower edges of the
robes; everything else is deep shadow. The wall and the door fade into black.

DO NOT: show any faces, add more candles, add color, add windows with
daylight, add readable text.
```
