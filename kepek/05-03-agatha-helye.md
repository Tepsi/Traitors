# 5. fejezet – A könyvtár

**Kapcsolódó mondat (5. fejezet, „A könyvtár"):**
„– Agatha helye – mondta Welles halkan."

**Jelenet:** a könyvtárban Mercury és Welles egy polc előtt áll, a szorosan sorakozó kötetek között egy karhossznyi, világosabb fájú rés. A háttérben a falnál fotelek, bennük ülő vendégek.

## Prompt (angolul)

```
Monochrome graphite pencil drawing on cream paper, hand-hatched, soft smudged
shading, fine paper grain, soft vignette edges, quiet, sorrowful and ominous
mood. Landscape, 3:2.

THE ROOM: a library in a Tudor castle. Heavy oak bookshelves rise from floor
to ceiling, packed with leather-bound volumes. In the LEFT wall, tall windows
let in grey fog light from the garden outside. Along the walls stand
upholstered armchairs.

COMPOSITION: camera at standing eye level, slightly behind and to the right of
the two men, so that we see them mostly from behind and in three-quarter
profile, looking at one tall oak bookshelf that fills the right two thirds of
the picture.

CENTER-RIGHT, in front of the shelf, close together: on the left of the pair,
Freddie Mercury, dark jacket, dark hair and moustache, his right arm extended
and the open hand pointing at the shelf, his left hand clenching into a fist
at his side; on the right of the pair, a step behind him, Orson Welles, in a
long dark coat, a solemn dignified figure, head slightly bowed, speaking
quietly, his face in three-quarter profile, solemn.

ON THE SHELF, at the exact height of Mercury's pointing hand: the books stand
shoulder to shoulder in tight rows on all the shelves, except for one gap
about an arm's length wide in the middle of one shelf. Inside the gap the
wood of the shelf board and the back panel is distinctly lighter and cleaner
than the dark, aged wood around it, as if a long row of books had stood there
for decades and the sun had never reached the wood behind them. The gap is
empty, the neighboring books lean slightly toward it but do not fill it.

BACKGROUND, LEFT, OUT OF FOCUS: the foggy windows and a few armchairs along
the wall. In the armchairs sit anonymous people in 1930s to 1970s clothes,
a few reading, a few whispering with their heads close together, hands in
laps. No faces are sharp.

LIGHT: soft grey light from the windows on the left falls across the shelf,
making the pale wood inside the gap the lightest area of the picture; the
two men are in half shadow.

DO NOT: fill the gap with books, make the gap only hand-wide, add color, add
readable text on the book spines, show any other person sharply, add more
than two main figures.
```

**Szereplők:** a nevek a promptban is szerepelnek.
