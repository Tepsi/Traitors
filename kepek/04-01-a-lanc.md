# 4. fejezet – A lánc

**Kapcsolódó mondat (4. fejezet, „A Kútház"):**
„Így állt fel a lánc."

**Jelenet:** a Kútház hátsó udvara oldalról nézve. A kútnál Brando áll, a kútházból a medencéig tizenöt ember és Welles alkot láncot, a vödrök kézről kézre járnak. A Játékmester a kútház kapujában áll.

## Prompt (angolul)

```
Monochrome graphite pencil drawing on cream paper, hand-hatched, soft smudged
shading, fine paper grain, soft vignette edges, ominous gothic mood.
Landscape, 3:2.

THE PLACE: the back courtyard of a Tudor castle, paved with wet grey flagstones,
fog pressing in from every side so that nothing beyond the courtyard walls is
visible. The ground slopes gently downward from LEFT to RIGHT.

COMPOSITION: a medium-high camera angle from the side of the courtyard, about
ten meters from the chain, looking across it, so that the whole chain is seen
in profile as a diagonal line running from the upper left to the lower right.

LEFT SIDE, on the higher ground: a small well house of grey stone with a heavy
timber beam roof, open on the front. In the dark opening a stone well rim, a
wooden pulley wheel with a rope hanging over it. Beside the well, along the
house wall, twenty plain wooden buckets stand in a neat row. Marlon Brando, in
a dark casual jacket, relaxed posture, one hand on the rope, stands at the
pulley with his back half turned to the camera, lowering a bucket into the
black well. In the shadow of the open doorway of the well house, behind
Brando, the very tall Game Master stands motionless in a floor-length black
cloak with a high stiff collar, face completely in shadow, long pale hands
folded.

CENTER: from the well the chain of people runs to the right across the flat
flagstones. Four people stand in a row, each passing a wooden bucket to the
next with both hands: first Ferenc Bessenyei, a broad imposing man in a dark
formal suit with silver hair; then Maria Callas in a dark cloak; then Salvador
Dalí with his long thin waxed upturned moustache; then Albert Einstein with
untidy white hair and a rumpled cardigan. Next to them, at the top of the
stairs, stands Louis de Funès, in a rumpled dark 1960s suit, steadying himself
with one hand on the wet wall.

RIGHT SIDE, lower ground: seven narrow wet stone steps, covered in moss,
descend from the center of the picture down to the right. On the steps and
along the path to the right stand the remaining people one behind another,
each about two meters from the next, all facing the same direction (to the
right, toward the basin), each handing a bucket to the one in front: Zsazsa
Gábor in a glamorous gown with many bracelets, Audrey Hepburn in a simple dark
1950s dress with short dark hair, Alfred Hitchcock in a formal dark
three-piece suit, Hofi Géza in a dark suit, Kabos Gyula in a dark 1930s suit
and bow tie, Freddie Mercury in a dark jacket with a moustache, Neumann János
in a precise dark business suit, Puskás Ferenc in a dark 1950s suit, Bud
Spencer very tall and bearded in an open-collared shirt, Elizabeth Taylor in
an evening gown with sparkling bracelets.

FAR RIGHT, at the lowest point: a wide copper basin on a low stone base beside
a wrought-iron garden gate set in a stone wall, the lowest of the rings carved
into the basin's side only a hand's height from the ground. Orson Welles
stands beside the basin, a heavy build in a long dark coat, with a metal
pouring jug in his hands, looking at the ground and at nobody.

LIGHT: dim, grey, even light from the fog, with long soft shadows; the buckets
and the wet steps catch the only highlights.

DO NOT: add more or fewer than seventeen people in total, make the chain
bend or double back, show the cloaked man's face, put the basin next to the
well, add color, add readable text.
```

**Szereplők:** a nevek a promptban is szerepelnek.
