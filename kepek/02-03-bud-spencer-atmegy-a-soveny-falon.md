# 2. fejezet – Bud Spencer áttör a sövényen

**Kapcsolódó mondat (2. fejezet, „A Holdvári-labirintus"):**
„A tiszafa először nem engedett, aztán mégis."

**Jelenet:** a labirintus közepéről nézzük. Bud Spencer éppen áttör az utolsó sövényfalon. Mögötte a három korábbi áttörés egyenes alagutat alkot a ködbe. Balra az előtérben az oszlop a pajzzsal és a haranggal.

## Prompt (angolul)

```
Monochrome graphite pencil drawing on cream paper, hand-hatched, soft smudged
shading, fine paper grain, soft vignette edges, ominous gothic mood with a
touch of dark humor. Landscape, 3:2.

COMPOSITION: the camera stands in the center of a hedge maze, in a round
grass clearing, at standing eye height, looking back along a long straight
line toward the maze wall.

FOREGROUND, LEFT: a stone column about two meters tall, large and close to
the camera, taking up the left fifth of the picture. On top of the column a
black iron shield with a crescent moon on its face is mounted. Beside the
shield a bronze bell hangs from an iron bracket on the column. The bell is
motionless.

CENTER: Bud Spencer, a very tall, powerfully built man with a thick dark beard, in a plain
open-collared light shirt with rolled sleeves, is bursting through the dense
hedge wall at the edge of the clearing, walking straight toward the camera,
shoulders first, unhurried, with a calm, almost bored expression, as if he
were pushing open a door. The yew branches around him snap and splinter, twigs
and leaves fly in the air, the hedge has been torn open in a ragged breach the
shape of his body.

BACKGROUND: behind him, going away from the camera in a perfectly straight
line, three more ragged breaches in three successive hedge walls form a
tunnel through the maze. The tunnel recedes in perspective and ends in drifting
fog. The straight tunnel makes it obvious that he walked a straight line
through four hedge walls. Left and right of the tunnel, dense dark hedge and
fog resting among the leaves.

LIGHT: pale, overcast, even light with the fog glowing at the far end of the
tunnel; the man and the splintering twigs are the darkest, sharpest part of
the picture.

DO NOT: show any other person, add color, make him angry or strained, add
readable text.
```

**Szereplők:** a nevek a promptban is szerepelnek.
