# 9. fejezet – Hat ártatlan

**Kapcsolódó mondat (9. fejezet, „A Kerekasztal"):**
„A füzetet az asztalra tette, és az ujja egy üres oldalra tévedt, aztán megállt."

**Jelenet:** a Kerekasztal vége után Zsazsa közelről: a kezén a karkötők, az ujja az asztalra tett füzet üres oldalán áll meg.

## Prompt (angolul)

```
Monochrome graphite pencil drawing on cream paper, hand-hatched, soft smudged
shading, fine paper grain, soft vignette edges, ominous gothic mood.
Landscape, 3:2.

THE SETTING: the edge of a huge round table of black oak in a domed stone
chamber, deep dark behind. On the polished table top, in the lower center of
the picture, lies a small open notebook with plain cream pages. The left page
is covered in short, tidy lines of tiny handwriting (illegible, only
scribbled texture, no readable letters). The right page is completely empty.

THE HAND: Gábor Zsazsa's right hand, slender, many thin sparkling bracelets
and a ring on it, lies on the notebook, the index finger resting on the empty
right page, stopped there, motionless, as if it had wandered onto it and
could not go on. A small flat piece of chalk and a slate lie beside the
notebook, the slate wiped clean.

THE WOMAN: seated just behind the notebook, in a glamorous 1950s gown with a
low neckline, her face turned toward the camera but lowered and slightly
out of focus, eyes cast down at the empty page, an empty, stunned
expression, no tears, no smile, dignified.

BACKGROUND: behind her, soft and dark, the rest of the table and a few
seated figures in shadow, unreadable; far behind them a tall black door with
a thin silver frame, closed.

COMPOSITION: close, camera at table height, slightly in front of the
notebook; the hand and the empty page in the exact center, the woman's face
in the upper third; shallow depth of field, edges fading into shadow.

LIGHT: one candle off to the right edge throws a warm low light across the
page and the bracelets, which catch small highlights; everything else falls
into soft black.

DO NOT: write readable text on the pages, show any other face sharply, show
blood or tears, add color, add more than one notebook.
```

**Szereplők:** Zsazsa (közelkép); a háttérben névtelen alakok.
