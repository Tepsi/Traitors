# 5. fejezet – A Vakfolyosó

**Kapcsolódó mondat (5. fejezet, „A Vakfolyosó"):**
„Brando kötötte be a szemét, aztán hosszasan állt, mintha a sötétben is látni akarna valamit."

**Jelenet:** a Nyugati Galéria közepén Brando bekötött szemmel áll, mozdulatlanul. Az erkélyről Hitchcock vezényli, két kézzel a korláton, mintha filmet rendezne.

## Prompt (angolul)

```
Monochrome graphite pencil drawing on cream paper, hand-hatched, soft smudged
shading, fine paper grain, soft vignette edges, ominous gothic mood.
Landscape, 3:2.

THE ROOM: a very long, narrow, dark gallery in a Tudor castle. The floor is
black and white marble tiles in an irregular scattered pattern, as if a
chessboard had been shuffled, the lines between the tiles barely visible.
Heavy dark velvet curtains cover the tall windows along one long wall. Along
the opposite long wall, high above the floor at the height of a first floor,
runs a narrow stone balcony with a stone balustrade. Tall candles in iron
holders stand along the walls, far apart.

COMPOSITION: camera at about the height of a standing person's chest, on the
curtained-window side of the gallery, looking slightly down and diagonally
across the floor toward the balcony wall. So in the picture the curtained
windows are behind the camera, the balcony is on the far side in the upper
left, and the gallery stretches away to the right into darkness.

CENTER OF THE PICTURE, on the marble floor, standing perfectly still: Marlon
Brando, in a dark casual jacket, relaxed posture, a light cloth tied over his
eyes, his face lifted a little and turned toward the balcony, his lips slightly
parted, his arms hanging loosely at his sides, his weight easy on both feet,
as if he were looking hard at something in the darkness although he cannot
see. He stands on a black tile; a white tile lies just in front of his shoes.
A small cloth purse lies on the marble a few steps to his right.

UPPER LEFT, ON THE BALCONY, directly above and slightly behind Brando:
Alfred Hitchcock, in a formal dark three-piece suit, standing upright, both
hands flat on the stone balustrade, his heavy head bent over the rail looking
down at Brando, a thin watchful half-smile on his lips, like a director
looking at a set through a viewfinder.

FAR RIGHT, SMALL AND NEARLY LOST IN THE DARK: a bronze bell hanging from a
thick rope and a plain wooden chair beside it, a very tall bearded man seated
on the chair with his hands folded in his lap, motionless.

LIGHT: dim and warm, a few candle pools; one soft light picks out Brando's
face and the white cloth over his eyes; the rest of the gallery falls into
deep shadow.

DO NOT: place the balcony on the same side as the windows, make the floor a
regular chessboard, show any other person's face, give Brando a hat or
sunglasses, add color, add readable text.
```

**Szereplők:** a nevek a promptban is szerepelnek.
