# 3. fejezet – Psota Irén a pajzzsal

**Kapcsolódó mondat (3. fejezet, „A Csend Szalonja"):**
„– Nem kell! – tiltakozott Psota Irén, és a pajzsot két kézzel tartotta, mintha kígyót fogna."

**Jelenet:** a Csend Szalonja. Psota Irén felállt a székéből, két kézzel, kinyújtott karral tartja a kis szürke vaspajzsot. Mögötte az óra, körülötte a székkör, a vendégek hideg arccal nézik.

## Prompt (angolul)

```
Monochrome graphite pencil drawing on cream paper, hand-hatched, soft smudged
shading, fine paper grain, soft vignette edges, tense and ominous mood.
Landscape, 3:2.

THE ROOM: a square salon with heavy dark green velvet curtains over the
windows, a thick dark patterned carpet and dark paneled walls. In the exact
center of the room stands a huge black grandfather clock, about three meters
tall, a silver pendulum visible behind a glass pane.

COMPOSITION: eye-level camera, slightly low, at the edge of a circle of
chairs, looking toward the center of the room. The clock is in the center of
the picture, tall and dark, and in front of it, a little to the left of
center, one woman stands in the middle of the circle.

THE WOMAN: Psota Irén, the Hungarian actress, a middle-aged woman in a 1960s dress, hair set in waves, has just
stood up out of her chair (the chair behind her, pushed back, empty). She
holds a small round grey iron shield with a single star engraved in raised
relief at its center, gripped with both hands at arm's length in front of her
body, held away from her as if it were a live snake. Her face is flushed, her
mouth wide open in protest, her eyes frightened. The whole body shouts.

NEXT TO HER: on the viewer's right, half a step behind her, a footman in a
black uniform and black peaked cap, who has just handed her the shield, stands
rigid, face neutral. At the other side of the clock, on the viewer's right,
a very tall man in a floor-length black cloak with a high stiff collar stands
motionless, face in deep shadow, no face visible.

THE CIRCLE: around the clock and the woman, in a ring, the other guests sit on
their chairs, all chairs facing inward, toward the center. About a dozen are
visible, in period clothing from the 1930s to the 1970s: they are all turned
toward the woman and stare at her with cold, still, expressionless faces. No
anger, no pity, no smile, only quiet arithmetic. Hands in laps. We see the
near ones from behind and the side, the far ones from the front.

LIGHT: strong diagonal light from the left, the woman lit, the stares in
shadow, the clock a black mass.

DO NOT: show anyone smiling, hold the shield like a weapon or like a prize,
show the cloaked man's face, add color, add readable text.
```

**Szereplők:** a nevek a promptban is szerepelnek.
