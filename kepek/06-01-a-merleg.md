# 6. fejezet – A Mérlegterem

**Kapcsolódó mondat (6. fejezet, „A Mérlegterem"):**
„A terem közepén egy sárgaréz mérleg állt, két serpenyővel, akkora, hogy egy ember is elfért volna benne."

**Jelenet:** a Keleti Szárny Mérleg-terme, még üresen, a próbatétel kezdete előtt. A terem közepén a hatalmas sárgaréz mérleg, az egyik serpenyő arany, a másik fekete vas, mindkettőn zárt fedél és egyetlen keskeny rés. A mérleg előtt lakkozott paraván, a terem szélén kis asztal tizenhárom szürke kővel. Az ajtónál a Játékmester áll.

## Prompt (angolul)

```
Monochrome graphite pencil drawing on cream paper, hand-hatched, soft smudged
shading, fine paper grain, soft vignette edges, ominous gothic mood.
Landscape, 3:2.

THE ROOM: a cold, tall, bare hall in the east wing of a Tudor castle. Dark
timber beams cross a high ceiling, the walls are rough grey stone, the floor
is dark flagstones. One tall narrow leaded window in the far wall lets in
pale grey fog light. A heavy oak door with iron straps stands in the short
wall at the LEFT edge of the picture.

COMPOSITION: camera on the long side of the room, at standing eye level,
looking straight across the width of the room. The picture is a wide,
balanced view with the scale in the exact center and a lot of cold empty
floor around it.

CENTER: an enormous brass scale on a tall brass column, so large that a
grown man could stand inside one of its pans. Its horizontal beam is held
perfectly level by a single iron pin driven through the middle of the
column. The two pans hang from chains at exactly the same height. The pan on
the LEFT, from the viewer, is bright gilded gold; the pan on the RIGHT is
black iron. Each pan is closed by a thick brass lid with one single narrow
slot, just wide enough for a stone to pass through, and the space under the
slot is pitch dark. Nothing is visible inside either pan. The brass catches
the grey window light in long soft highlights.

ON THE LEFT OF THE SCALE, between the scale and the oak door: a tall, slim,
black lacquered folding screen, taller than a man, seen almost edge-on so it
reads as a narrow dark upright panel; its wide face is turned toward the
door, so nobody coming in from the door could see the scale behind it. A
short distance of empty floor separates the screen from the scale.

AT THE FAR WALL, on the RIGHT, below the window: a small plain wooden table
with exactly thirteen smooth, plain grey stones, each the size of a fist,
set in one neat straight row along the table, evenly spaced.

BESIDE THE OAK DOOR, at the far LEFT edge of the picture: the Game Master, a
very tall gaunt man in a floor-length black cloak with a high stiff collar,
his face in deep shadow and hidden by the collar, long pale hands folded in
front of him, standing motionless as a statue. He is small next to the
scale, which shows its size.

LIGHT: cold grey fog light from the window, soft long shadows across the
flagstones, the brass and the lacquer screen the brightest things in the
picture.

DO NOT: add any people except the Game Master, open the lids, show anything
inside the pans, put more or fewer than thirteen stones on the table, show
the Game Master's face, add color, add readable text or marks on the scale.
```

**Szereplők:** csak a Játékmester szerepel, a nevek nem fontosak.
