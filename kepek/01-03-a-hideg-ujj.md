# 1. fejezet – A hideg ujj

**Kapcsolódó mondat (1. fejezet, „A Nagyterem"):**
„Aztán hideg ujj érintette a vállát, könnyedén, szinte gyengéden."

**Jelenet:** a vendégek lehunyt szemmel ülnek a sötét Nagyteremben. A kamera az asztal másik oldaláról nézi őket. A középső vendég mögött áll a Játékmester, és két ujjal megérinti a vállát. Az érintett vendég a szövegben nincs megnevezve, ezért a képen is névtelen.

## Prompt (angolul)

```
Monochrome graphite pencil drawing on cream paper, hand-hatched, soft smudged
shading, fine paper grain, soft vignette edges, ominous gothic mood, strong
chiaroscuro. Landscape, 3:2.

COMPOSITION: medium shot. The camera is on one long side of a banquet table at
chest height, slightly low, looking straight across the table at five guests
who sit in a row on the far side. They all face the camera. In the foreground,
along the bottom edge, the white linen of the tablecloth, and the dark
silhouettes of two silver candlesticks whose candles are out, thin smoke still
rising.

THE ROW OF GUESTS (from the viewer's left to right), all with their eyes
tightly shut, hands resting on the table in front of them:
1. a woman in a tailored 1930s dark outfit with a fur coat over her shoulders,
   sitting perfectly rigid and motionless like a statue;
2. a woman in an evening gown with many sparkling bracelets, her hands on the
   table slightly trembling so that the jewelry seems to shiver;
3. IN THE CENTER: a man in a dark suit with his head bowed a little and his
   shoulders drawn up, eyes shut, face tense. He is the one who is being
   touched;
4. a man in a dark 1930s suit and bow tie, eyes shut, lips moving silently as
   if he were praying;
5. a man in a light 1950s suit, who smiled all evening, now serious, eyes
   shut.

THE CLOAKED MAN: directly behind the center guest stands a very tall man in a
floor-length black cloak with a high stiff collar, his face lost in deep
shadow (no face visible). One long pale hand reaches forward and rests two
fingers very lightly on the center guest's right shoulder (on the viewer's
left side of the man). That touching hand is the brightest, most detailed part
of the picture, the center of the composition.

BACKGROUND: directly behind the row of guests, a dark oak-paneled wall, and in
the wall, behind the cloaked man's shoulder, one tall narrow window with pale
foggy moonlight, the only light. The rest is almost black.

DO NOT: show the cloaked man's face, open anyone's eyes, add candle flames,
add color, add readable text.
```

**Szereplők (név szerint, ha a generátor engedi):** balról jobbra Marlene Dietrich, Elizabeth Taylor, a középső névtelen, Kabos Gyula, Louis Armstrong.
