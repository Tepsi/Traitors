# 1. fejezet – A hideg ujj

**Kapcsolódó mondat (1. fejezet, „A Nagyterem"):**
„Aztán hideg ujj érintette a vállát, könnyedén, szinte gyengéden."

**Jelenet:** a vendégek lehunyt szemmel ülnek a sötét Nagyteremben. A kamera közvetlenül a Játékmester háta mögül néz előre. A hatalmas fekete köpeny teljesen eltakarja az érintett vendéget. Csak a halvány kéz látszik, amint két ujjal megérint egy sötét ruhadarabot, a vállnak egy keskeny részletét. A szöveg nem mondja meg, ki az érintett, ezért a képen sem derülhet ki: se a neme, se az arca, se a haja.

## Prompt (angolul)

```
Monochrome graphite pencil drawing on cream paper, hand-hatched, soft smudged
shading, fine paper grain, soft vignette edges, ominous gothic mood, strong
chiaroscuro. Landscape, 3:2.

COMPOSITION: an over-the-shoulder shot from directly behind a very tall figure
in a floor-length black cloak with a high stiff collar and wide sleeves. The
camera is low and close behind him. His back and the heavy folds of the
cloak fill the whole left half and the center of the picture as one huge,
almost completely black shape. We see him only from behind: no face, no
profile, nothing of his head but the dark hood-like collar. The cloak
completely hides whoever sits in front of him: the seated person is entirely
behind the black cloth and cannot be seen at all, neither head, hair, neck,
face, body, nor chair.

THE TOUCH: from the wide sleeve a long, pale, slender hand reaches forward
and down past the edge of the cloak at the center right of the picture. Two
of its fingers rest very lightly on the corner of a plain dark shoulder, just
a small fragment of dark fabric that peeks out beside the cloak. The
fragment is cropped: no collar, no neck, no hair, no jewelry, no tie, no
detail at all that shows who or what kind of person it is. The hand and its
two fingers are the brightest, most detailed part of the whole picture and
the center of the composition.

BEYOND, to the right and above the cloak: a long banquet table covered with
white linen, and across it, on the far side, four guests who sit in a row and
face the camera, softly lit and a little out of focus, all with their eyes
tightly shut and their hands on the table:
- a woman in a tailored 1930s dark outfit with a fur coat over her shoulders,
  perfectly rigid, like a statue;
- a woman in an evening gown with many sparkling bracelets, her hands slightly
  trembling so that the jewelry seems to shiver;
- a man in a dark 1930s suit and bow tie, lips moving silently as if
  praying;
- a man in a light 1950s suit, who smiled all evening, now serious.
On the table, between the guests and the cloaked figure, the dark silhouettes
of silver candlesticks whose candles are out, with thin smoke still rising.

BACKGROUND: behind the row of guests, a dark oak-paneled wall with one tall
narrow window with pale foggy moonlight, the only light in the picture. The
rest is almost black.

DO NOT: show the face or head of the cloaked figure, show the seated person
who is being touched (not even partly: only the one fragment of shoulder
fabric), show anyone's open eyes, add candle flames, add color, add readable
text.
```

**Szereplők (név szerint, ha a generátor engedi):** az asztal túloldalán, balról jobbra Marlene Dietrich, Elizabeth Taylor, Kabos Gyula, Louis Armstrong. Az érintett szándékosan nem látszik.
