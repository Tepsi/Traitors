# 5. fejezet – A Vakfolyosó

**Kapcsolódó mondat (5. fejezet, „A Vakfolyosó"):**
„A padlója fekete és fehér márványlapokból állt, szabálytalan mintában, mintha egy sakktáblát összekevertek volna, és a lapok között alig látszott a vonal."

**Jelenet:** a Nyugati Galéria, a kastély leghosszabb és legsötétebb terme. Az első pár indul: Einstein bekötött szemmel halad a márványon, Neumann az erkélyről vezeti. A terem végén a harang és a szék, rajta Bud Spencer.

## Prompt (angolul)

```
Monochrome graphite pencil drawing on cream paper, hand-hatched, soft smudged
shading, fine paper grain, soft vignette edges, ominous gothic mood.
Landscape, 3:2.

THE ROOM: a very long, narrow, dark gallery in a Tudor castle, much longer
than it is wide, so that the far end dissolves into darkness. The floor is
made of black and white marble tiles in an irregular scattered pattern, as if
a chessboard had been shuffled, the lines between the tiles barely visible.
Heavy dark velvet curtains cover the tall windows along the RIGHT long wall,
no daylight comes in. Along the walls stand tall candles in iron holders, so
far apart that their light pools never touch each other. Along the LEFT long
wall, high above the floor at about the height of a first floor, runs a narrow
stone balcony with a stone balustrade, from which the whole floor can be
overlooked.

COMPOSITION: low camera at the entrance end of the gallery, at about the
height of a standing person's chest, looking straight down the length of the
room, so that the tiles recede in strong perspective toward the far end. The
balcony is on the left of the picture, the curtained windows on the right.

FOREGROUND: the irregular black and white tiles, large and sharp. A few small
cloth purses lie scattered on the marble, drawn simply as little rounded
bundles with a drawstring, one in the foreground left, one near the right
wall, two further down the floor.

MIDGROUND, slightly right of the center line: Albert Einstein, untidy white
hair, a rumpled cardigan, a dark cloth tied over his eyes, walking very
carefully in small short steps, both arms held a little forward and away from
his sides, one foot just lifted to take the next step, his head tilted a
little to the left toward the voice. He is small against the long room.

LEFT, HIGH ON THE BALCONY, at about the middle of the room: Neumann János, in
a precise dark business suit, leaning on the stone balustrade with both
forearms, looking down at Einstein, his mouth slightly open as if counting.

FARTHEST, AT THE END OF THE GALLERY, small and almost lost in the dark: a
single bronze bell hanging from a thick rope, and beside it a plain wooden
chair. On the chair sits Bud Spencer, very tall and powerful, with a thick
dark beard, in an open-collared light shirt, his hands folded in his lap,
perfectly motionless and silent.

Further along the LEFT balcony, farther from the camera and smaller, stands
the Game Master, a very tall gaunt figure in a floor-length black cloak with
a high stiff collar, his face in deep shadow, long pale hands on the
balustrade, motionless.

LIGHT: dim and warm, only the sparse candles; the white tiles catch a little
light and the black tiles swallow it; long soft shadows run along the floor.

DO NOT: make the floor a regular chessboard, show daylight, place windows on
the left wall, add more than the figures named, show the Game Master's face,
put a blindfold on anyone but Einstein, add color, add readable text.
```

**Szereplők:** a nevek a promptban is szerepelnek.
